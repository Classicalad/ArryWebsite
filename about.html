The Baker

Hi, I'm Arry.

What started as weekend baking experiments turned into a full-blown obsession with flavour, texture and the look on people's faces when they take that first bite. Every cake I make is handcrafted in small batches using seasonal ingredients, no shortcuts, no box mixes — just honest, delicious baking.

Fennel Heads is my little corner of the internet where I share what I'm making. Whether you're after a showstopper celebration cake, a simple birthday treat, or a tub of home-churned ice cream, I'd love to bake something special for you.
