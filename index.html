The Fennel Heads difference

Made with care, every time

Small Batch

Nothing is ever mass-produced or pre-made. Every order is crafted in small quantities for maximum quality.

Fresh to Order

Your cake is baked and your ice cream is churned only after you order — never frozen in advance, never stale.

Toronto Made

Everything is made in our home kitchen in Toronto with quality, locally sourced ingredients wherever possible.

Dietary Friendly

Gluten-free, nut-free, and vegan options are available on request. Just mention your needs when ordering.
